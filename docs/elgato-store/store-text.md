## Description (English, 250–1,500 characters)

Paste it with the editor's Markdown mode (M↓) switched on.

```
Keeps your computer awake while you are away — from one key. Press it and a hamster starts running: the screen does not lock, the computer does not sleep, and the mouse gets a tiny nudge on a timer so chat and meeting apps keep showing you as present. Press again to stop.

**Always turns itself off** after the time you choose — 15 minutes to 4 hours, never longer, so a computer is never left awake and unlocked for good. The key counts down beside its orange BRB tab, and a hamster in the system tray shows that it is on, with Stop in its menu.

**Your pace:** Zen (invisible 1-pixel moves), Nudge, Circle or Random — or stay awake without moving the mouse at all. Move every 15 seconds to 10 minutes, or at random. Every move ends where it started. Test the mouse with one click in the settings.

**Light on your system:** Windows' own stay-awake call (the same as PowerToys Awake) and small mouse moves — no admin rights, no settings changed, no autostart, no network. Everything stops with the Stream Deck app.

When it is off, the hamster sleeps on the key.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11, Stream Deck 7.1 or newer. Also available for OpenDeck on Linux.

Made by Idus with AI assistance (Claude). Some workplaces and games do not allow tools that simulate activity — check before you use it there.
```
